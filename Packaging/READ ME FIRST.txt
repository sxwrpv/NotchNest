NotchNest — install in three steps
==================================

Needs: an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 14 Sonoma or later,
about 4 GB of free disk space, and internet for the first launch.

1. Drag NotchNest onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open it. NotchNest isn't notarized by Apple, so the first open is blocked:
     • macOS 15 Sequoia / 26 Tahoe: click "Done", then open
       System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to
       "NotchNest was blocked…" and click "Open Anyway".
     • macOS 14 Sonoma: right-click NotchNest → Open → Open.
   (Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/NotchNest.app )
   Next time, skip this step entirely with the one-line install in Terminal:
     curl -fsSL https://github.com/sxwrpv/NotchNest/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash

3. The setup assistant does the rest on its own:
     • installs a private Python and the dictation engine (~300 MB)
     • picks the right speech and AI models for your Mac's memory and
       downloads them (~2.5 GB) — after that everything runs offline
     • walks you through Microphone, Accessibility and Calendar access
     • turns on "Open at login"

Dictate: hold RIGHT OPTION (⌥) and talk, or double-tap it to toggle.
The text is copied to your clipboard and shown in the notch.
Hover the notch to open it; the menu-bar icon has Settings.

Uninstall: quit NotchNest, delete it from Applications, then remove
  ~/Library/Application Support/NotchNest
  ~/Library/Caches/NotchNest
  ~/.murmur
  ~/.cache/huggingface/hub/models--mlx-community--*   (the downloaded models)
